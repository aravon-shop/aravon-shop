Contact Aravon Shop

Welcome to the Aravon Shop Customer Support page. We are committed to providing a helpful, reliable, and straightforward shopping experience. If you have a question about an order, payment, delivery, product, account, refund, or another part of your shopping experience, our support team is here to assist you.

Our recommended way to contact Customer Support

If you have an Aravon Shop customer account, we recommend contacting our support team through the Support / Messages area inside your account.

Sending your message from your account helps us connect your request with your customer information and, when applicable, your order details. This can make it easier for our support team to understand your request and provide the appropriate assistance.

Contact Us Through Your Account

For most customer service questions, please sign in to your Aravon Shop account and use the available Support or Messages section.

This is especially useful when you are contacting us about an existing order, delivery, product issue, account question, or another request related to your customer account.

💬 Why use Account Support?

Your support conversation can remain connected to your customer account, making it easier for our team to review the relevant information and communicate with you.

When contacting us about an order, please include your order number whenever possible. This helps us locate the correct order and review the situation more efficiently.

Please do not include sensitive payment information such as your full card number, security code, or account passwords in a support message.

Refunds, Payments & Order Assistance

Our support team can assist with questions and requests involving refunds, payments, orders, delivery problems, damaged or incorrect products, and other customer service matters.

If your request concerns a refund or another issue that requires additional review, please provide as much relevant information as possible. Depending on the circumstances, our team may need to review the order details before determining the appropriate next step.

A refund request does not automatically guarantee that a refund will be issued. Refunds and other resolutions are handled according to the applicable order information and Aravon Shop policies.

Email Support

If you are unable to access your customer account or need to contact us by email, you can reach the Aravon Shop support team at the address below.

Customer Support Email
support@aravonshop.com

When contacting us by email, please provide enough information for us to understand your request. For order-related questions, including your order ID and the email address associated with your purchase can help us identify the relevant order.

What to Include in Your Message

Providing complete and accurate information helps our support team understand your request and respond more effectively. Where applicable, please include:

  • Your full name
  • Your order ID (for example: order_1721670000003)
  • The email address associated with your Aravon Shop account or order
  • A clear description of the issue or question
  • Relevant product information such as the product name or item involved
  • Screenshots or photographs when they help explain a product or order issue
  • Any relevant tracking information if your request concerns delivery

Support Response Time

Our team aims to review customer support requests as promptly as possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request, the information provided, and the volume of support enquiries we are handling.

During busy periods, weekends, public holidays, or periods of unusually high customer demand, a response may take longer than usual.

To help avoid unnecessary delays, please avoid sending multiple duplicate requests about the same issue. If you have already contacted us, replying to the existing conversation is usually the best way to keep the information together.

Our Commitment to You

At Aravon Shop, we believe good customer service is an important part of a positive shopping experience. We aim to communicate clearly, treat customer requests respectfully, and provide helpful information when assistance is needed.

Whether you need help understanding an order, checking delivery information, reporting a problem with a product, asking about a payment, or discussing a refund request, we encourage you to contact us with the relevant details so our team can properly review your request.
