Privacy Policy

At Aravon Shop, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you shop with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to process your orders and provide a smooth shopping experience. This includes:

  • Personal details: name, email address, phone number
  • Order information: products purchased, order ID, payment status
  • Shipping details: address, postal code, city, country
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for legitimate ecommerce purposes, including:

  • Processing and delivering your orders
  • Sending order confirmations and tracking updates
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving our website and services
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring secure transactions

3. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely using industry‑standard protection methods. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except when required to complete your order (e.g., shipping carriers).

4. Payment Information

Aravon Shop does not store full payment card details. All payments are processed securely through trusted payment providers.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your shopping experience, such as remembering your cart or keeping you logged in. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Ask us to update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your data (where legally allowed)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can contact our support team:

Email: privacy@aravonshop.com

We aim to respond to all privacy‑related inquiries within 24–48 hours.

Thank you for trusting Aravon Shop. Your privacy and security are always our priority.
